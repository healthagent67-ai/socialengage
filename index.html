Frequently Asked Questions

Will I own the website?+

Yes, 100%. Your website is yours from day one. A lot of web agencies use a model where you lose all access to your website if you cancel, and this can have a devastating effect on your business. With SEM, if you choose to cancel, you keep your website.

Do I pay anything before I see my design?+

No. We mock up your site first and refine it with you. You pay the $500 only after you approve your design, and you're covered by our 100% satisfaction guarantee.

How can you build a $5,000-quality site for $500?+

No bloated process, no months of back-and-forth, no agency overhead. We focus on what makes a small-business site perform: a clean custom design, fast mobile-friendly pages, clear calls to action, and SEO basics.

What's the difference between the two hosting plans?+

Both include unlimited updates. On the $20/mo plan, you host the site on your own hosting account and we handle every update. On the $50/mo plan, we host it for you too, so there's nothing to manage.

How long is your process?+

We understand a lot of web design agencies can take months to complete your site. We typically launch our client's site within two weeks, approved by our clients with our satisfaction guarantee.
